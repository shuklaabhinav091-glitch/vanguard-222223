THE CRISIS

CRUSHER DUST: A SILENT EPIDEMIC

In 200–300 TPH stone crushers, the jaw-crusher discharge zone sees PM2.5/PM10 levels 10–20x above DGMS legal limits. Workers face irreversible silicosis — a fatal, preventable lung disease.

Conventional / unregulated methods (crude pipe sprinklers) flood sites with 9 million+ litres of water annually yet fail to capture respirable dust. High-end fog cannons cost crores and remain energy-inefficient.

⚠ NGT penalties now reach ₹5 crore per violation. Maharashtra Pollution Control Board has shut down multiple plants — compliance is mandatory, not optional.
